🤖 The ML Stack

Tier 1 — HuggingFace

Uses the cardiffnlp/twitter-roberta-base-emotion RoBERTa model from Hugging Face for advanced emotion classification.

Tier 2 — Logistic Regression

A lightweight sklearn-based classifier trained on handcrafted linguistic and sentiment features.

Tier 3 — Rule-Based

Fallback keyword and sentiment-based emotion detection system with negation handling and polarity scoring.

About Reflectra 💭

Reflectra bridges therapeutic journaling with applied machine learning. Every entry runs through a sentiment pipeline that classifies emotion, extracts themes, surfaces similar memories, and updates your mood forecast — all while the analytics dashboard tracks your emotional trajectory over time.
